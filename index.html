About Us

Who We Are

Brahmi Eduhub Services is an authorized franchise of Mechatron Robotics, dedicated to bringing world-class STEM and robotics education to students in our community.

Robotics Training

Hands-on robotics courses designed for school students from Grade 1 to 12, covering fundamentals to advanced programming.

STEM Education

Competitions

Our Programs

Courses We Offer

Age-appropriate, curriculum-aligned programs that build critical thinking, creativity, and technical skills.

Level 1

Junior Innovators

Grades 1 – 3

Introduction to basic robotics, simple machines, and block-based coding through playful activities.

  • Block coding basics
  • Simple motor & sensor projects
  • Team-building challenges
Level 2

Robotics Explorers

Grades 4 – 7

Build and program autonomous robots using Arduino and sensors. Learn logic, loops, and problem-solving.

  • Arduino programming
  • Sensor integration
  • Line-following & obstacle bots
Level 3

Tech Masters

Grades 8 – 12

Advanced robotics, Python, IoT, and competitive programming. Project-based learning with real-world applications.

  • Python & IoT
  • Advanced sensor fusion
  • Competition-ready projects
